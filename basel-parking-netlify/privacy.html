P Basel Parking

Privacy and data

Basel Parking displays public parking information. No accounts or reservations.

Storage on this device

Your storage choice is kept locally. Accepting also saves your language and public historical readings. You can refuse or change your choice on the home page.

Services contacted

Netlify hosts this site. Your browser contacts data.bs.ch for parking data. These services receive your connection information, including your IP address. No analytics script or advertising is included.

External links

Directions opens Google Maps. Official details opens the Basel parking guidance website. Their own privacy policies apply.

Data limitations

Data can arrive late. Estimates are not reservations. Regular hours and height limits are maintained manually.
